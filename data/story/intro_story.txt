In a world shaped by small choices and quiet places,
something has begun to stir.

Not with fire or thunder,
but with moments easily missed.

Old paths are being walked again.
New ones are waiting to be found.

This is not a story of destiny,
or of heroes chosen long ago.

It is a story of ordinary beginnings,
and what happens when someone decides to take the next step.

The world is open.
The story is unfinished.

And it starts now.
